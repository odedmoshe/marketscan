• The WordPress population figures cover {{N_COLLECTED}} of the directory's 71,091 listings ({{COVERAGE}}%), not all of them. Enumeration needs two passes over two different orderings because browse=popular both repeats and skips at depth, and because it ranks only 66,998 of the 71,091 — omitting, by construction, the plugins with no install base. The residual gap is concentrated in older, low-install listings, which is the direction that makes the staleness figure a floor rather than a ceiling.
  • The author field does not resolve corporate ownership. Plugins are grouped by the API's author string, so a conglomerate publishing under several brand names is counted as several independent authors. Search & Replace Everything by WPCode is an Awesome Motive property that this method scores as a first-time author. Every such miss inflates the independent count, so “{{BT_NONE}} breakthroughs from authors with nothing behind them” is an upper bound.
  • The zero-install column has a recency confound and it is not fully removable. Newer plugins have had less time to accumulate installs, so part of the rise is mechanical. The comparison that carries weight is the 2024 cohort at {{Z2024}}%, which has had eighteen months to two and a half years, against the 2019 cohort at {{Z2019}}%. The monthly submission counts have no such confound.
  • A third figure has been withdrawn. “Only 15.1% carry the Built for Shopify badge” does not survive re-measurement — a whole-store sample returns {{SHOP_BFS}}%, and thirty apps re-checked from the original sample disagreed on fourteen, always in the same direction. It is withdrawn rather than corrected because the collector that produced it was never saved, so the disagreement cannot be diagnosed. Every collector behind the figures on this page is now in research/ in the repository, which is the actual fix.
